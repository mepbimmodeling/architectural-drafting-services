Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What architectural drafting services do you provide for US projects?
We provide a comprehensive range of architectural services tailored for the US market, including 2D AutoCAD drafting, full Construction Documentation (CD) sets, 3D BIM modeling (Revit), MEP coordination, and photorealistic 3D rendering.
Do you follow AIA layering standards and US building codes?
Absolutely. All our drafting outputs strictly adhere to the National CAD Standards (NCS) and American Institute of Architects (AIA) layering conventions. We draft with a deep understanding of the International Building Code (IBC) and federal ADA guidelines.
What software platforms does your team utilize?
Our core tech stack includes Autodesk AutoCAD, Revit, Navisworks (for clash detection), and SketchUp. For rendering and visualizations, we utilize 3ds Max, V-Ray, Enscape, and Lumion to ensure industry-leading output quality.
Can you match our firm's specific CAD templates and title blocks?
Yes, this is our standard procedure. During onboarding, we ingest your firm's specific CAD templates, title blocks, Plot Styles (.ctb/.stb), and custom dynamic blocks to ensure our deliverables look exactly as if they were drafted by your in-house team.
What is the typical turnaround time for a standard residential CD set?
Turnaround times depend strictly on the project's scale and LOD requirements. However, a standard single-family residential Construction Document (CD) set typically takes 1 to 2 weeks. We also offer expedited tracking for firm deadlines.
How accurate are your Point Cloud to CAD conversion services?
Our scan-to-CAD models achieve up to ±5mm accuracy, depending on the quality of the original point cloud data (typically provided in .rcp or .e57 formats). We carefully trace wall thicknesses, structural elements, and MEP fixtures to capture true "as-built" conditions.
How do you handle design changes and revisions?
We expect iterative design changes. We establish a structured revision cycle, utilizing cloud-based markup tools like Bluebeam Revu or Autodesk Construction Cloud. Your redlines are implemented swiftly, and the models are republished for your review.
